About Us

Queens-Born. Roof-Obsessed.
Family-Owned Since 2008.

PeakBuild Solutions wasn't built in a boardroom. It started with a truck, a crew of two, and a promise to never cut corners.

PeakBuild Solutions was founded in 2008 by Gadel David, a Queens native who spent a decade working under some of the best roofers in New York before deciding homeowners deserved better — better communication, better materials, and a contractor who actually stands behind their work.

What started as a two-man operation out of Jamaica, Queens has grown into a trusted team of 14 licensed professionals serving all five boroughs. We've completed over 1,500 roofing projects — from single-family homes in Flushing to multi-unit properties in Astoria — and every single one comes with our 10-year workmanship guarantee.

We're not a franchise. We're not a call center that dispatches strangers. When you call PeakBuild, you get us — the same crew, the same standards, every time.

Built for New York weather. Queens roofs take a beating — brutal freeze-thaw winters, heavy summer storms, and punishing humidity cycles. Every roof we install is spec'd specifically for NYC's climate using materials rated for extreme temperature swings, high wind loads, and heavy snow accumulation. Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are completed in one day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two days. We'll give you an accurate timeline in your quote.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We handle the entire claims process for you. Documentation, adjuster meetings, and final paperwork. Many of our jobs are fully covered by insurance.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners throughout the region. Give us a call with your address and we'll confirm if we can come to you — usually yes.

Is the estimate really free?

Completely free, no strings attached. We'll come out, inspect your roof, and give you a full written quote. You decide if you want to move forward.

What warranty do you offer?

We offer a 10-year workmanship warranty on all installations, plus the manufacturer's material warranty which typically runs 25–50 years depending on the shingle line.

Can you handle emergency repairs?

Yes. If you have active storm damage or a leak, call us directly and we'll prioritize your home. Same-day emergency tarping is available when needed.
